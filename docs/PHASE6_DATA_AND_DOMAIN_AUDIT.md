## 1. Executive Summary

Before implementing any chemistry adaptation or transfer learning algorithms in Phase 6, a rigorous data origin and domain audit was executed across all available specimens. 

The audit established that:
1. The **Real Physical Cohort** consists strictly of $N = 12$ physical battery modules/cells:
   - $8$ Lithium Iron Phosphate (LFP) cells (`SPECIMEN_01` to `SPECIMEN_08`)
   - $3$ Nickel Manganese Cobalt (NMC) cells (`SPECIMEN_09` to `SPECIMEN_11`)
   - $1$ UNKNOWN / Exotic experimental chemistry cell (`SPECIMEN_12_UNKNOWN_EXOTIC`)
2. The **Synthetic Simulation Cohort** consists of $N = 100$ cells drawn from the calibrated physics simulator:
   - $50$ synthetic LFP specimens
   - $50$ synthetic NMC specimens
3. The **Total Computational Cohort** equals $N = 112$ specimens ($12$ physical, $100$ synthetic).
4. Physical NMC evidence ($N = 3$) is severely underpowered ($1 - \beta < 0.20$ for modest effect sizes). Any cross-chemistry claims derived from physical NMC cells are strictly designated **EXPLORATORY**.
5. The single unmodeled cell (`SPECIMEN_12`) must permanently remain classified as **UNKNOWN**; it cannot be coerced into LFP or NMC.
6. RUL targets are **strictly unavailable** on this cross-sectional screening cohort; RUL cross-domain validation is explicitly declared **NOT PERFORMED**.

## 3. Chemistry Label Provenance System

To prevent synthetic simulations from being conflated with physical measurements, every chemistry assignment is assigned a strict provenance tag:

| Provenance State | Formal Definition | Applied To | Trust Level |
|---|---|---|:---:|
| **VERIFIED** | Direct laboratory analytical verification (XRF, dQ/dV spectroscopy, thermodynamic OCV profiling) | `SPECIMEN_01`, `03`–`08`, `09`–`11` | High (Level 2) |
| **DOCUMENTED** | Manufacturer intake datasheet or physical qualification tag | `SPECIMEN_02_HEALTHY_LFP_ALT` | Medium-High |
| **INFERRED** | Bayesian posterior update from operational voltage/slope telemetry | Runtime disambiguation output | Conditional |
| **SIMULATED** | Generated by numerical differential equation solver / ECM simulation | All 100 synthetic specimens | Level 1 Only |
| **UNKNOWN** | Unmodeled, unverified, or exotic experimental chemistry | `SPECIMEN_12_UNKNOWN_EXOTIC` | Zero (Gated) |

> **Critical Rule**: A `SIMULATED` chemistry label is NEVER treated as equivalent to `PHYSICALLY VERIFIED` chemistry. `SPECIMEN_12` must never be coerced into LFP or NMC merely because its resting voltage falls near a specific plateau.

## 6. Audit Verdict on Data Sufficiency

> **Primary Data Sufficiency Decision**:  
> **NO — The current physical dataset ($N=8$ LFP, $N=3$ NMC, $N=1$ UNKNOWN) is INSUFFICIENT to establish statistically defensible physical cross-chemistry generalization.**
>
> With only $N=3$ physical NMC specimens, any cross-chemistry transfer model has insufficient statistical degrees of freedom ($df = 1$ in NMC-test folds). While synthetic cohorts ($N=100$) can explore mathematical transfer bounds and failure modes, they constitute Level 1 evidence and cannot substitute for physical cross-chemistry validation.
