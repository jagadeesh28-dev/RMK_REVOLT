## 2. Procurement Summary & Safety Integrity
1. **Low Voltage Compliance:** Maximum total string voltage is $4 \times 3.65\text{V} = 14.6\text{V}$, remaining strictly below the $60\text{V}$ Extra-Low Voltage (SELV) hazardous shock threshold.
2. **True Component Sourcing:** Every listed component is commercially available from verified Indian distributors with 3-day surface delivery to Chennai/Tamil Nadu.
3. **Safety Redundancy:** No single electrical failure (MOSFET short, sensor bias, ESP32 crash, or resistor overheating) can cause an uncontrolled current or thermal condition without being intercepted by the sacrificial fuse, the bimetallic thermal snap, or the LM393 analog comparator.
