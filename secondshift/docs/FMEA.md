## 2. Summary of Fail-Safe Invariants
1. **Zero-Power Fail-Safe:** Loss of 12V contactor bus, 5V comparator supply, or 3.3V logic supply automatically drops contactor drive, de-energizing the power loop within $< 15\text{ ms}$.
2. **Firmware Disobedience:** Even if the ESP32 commands `RELAY_ON` and `GATE_SERIES_ON`, an analog trip from LM393 or mechanical trip from KSD9700 will physically shunt the drive line to ground, cutting power regardless of MCU code.
3. **Epistemic Invariant:** When chemistry identity is not proven with $\ge 99.0\%$ certainty, autonomous load-bearing deployment is structurally impossible.
